# Corrections

## 2026-10-27 — errata to planar-transport-map-sensitivity-certificates 0.2.0-candidate (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23205731)

The archived PDF, the manuscript source, the Zenodo deposit and the tag `v0.2.0-candidate` are unchanged. These errata were confirmed by the Evidence Press archive audit and are also shown at <https://evidencepress.org/releases/planar-transport-map-sensitivity-certificates/>.

1. Corrected a convergence statement in §5. The archived text says the adverse family converges within 256 sub-boxes. Table 2 shows that it narrows to a relative gap of about 10⁻³ (upper bound to supremum ratio 1.0011–1.0014) and, as §3.3 says, never converges exactly. The text should read: “the one-parameter tilt family converges within three sub-boxes, and the adverse family narrows to a relative gap of about 10⁻³ within 256”.

   The certified bounds in Table 2 are correct. No theorem, certificate, computation or stated result is affected.
